## Day 1 — The Cybersecurity Landscape

**Core idea:** Cybersecurity is just the digital version of the physical security you already practice (locking your door, not leaving windows open) — protecting computers, networks, data, and people from digital attacks. The lesson also introduced the two entry-level career paths this program builds toward: **SOC Analyst** (monitoring a SIEM dashboard, investigating alerts, detective work) and **GRC Analyst** (checking compliance like GDPR, assessing vendor risk, writing policy — less technical, more analytical).

**Case study — Colonial Pipeline:** On May 7, 2021, a single compromised employee password (no two-factor auth) let attackers lock Colonial Pipeline out of its own systems and demand ransom. Result: the pipeline shut down for 6 days, gas stations ran dry across multiple states, and Colonial paid $4.4 million in ransom. The lesson's point: most attacks aren't sophisticated — they exploit basic mistakes — which is exactly why the fundamentals taught in this program are valuable.

**Quiz takeaway:** Cybersecurity, in simple terms, is protecting computers, networks, and data from attacks (not building software or fixing machines). Most cyberattacks start with something simple, like a weak password or a suspicious email.
